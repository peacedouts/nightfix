# Trademark policy

The code is opensource under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The name **Nightfix** and the Nightfix logo are trademarks of the project's maintainer and aren't covered by that license (Apache-2.0 §6). This policy says how you can use them.

## Fine without asking

- Saying your product or service **works with**, **integrates with** or **is built on** Nightfix.
- Running Nightfix, including modified versions, inside your organization, and calling it Nightfix internally.
- Redistributing **unmodified** Nightfix releases under the Nightfix name.
- Writing about Nightfix, teaching it, and offering services to install or support it, as long as you describe them accurately ("Nightfix setup by Acme", not "Nightfix Support").

## Please don't

- Use **Nightfix** (or a confusingly similar name) as the name of a fork, a hosted service, or a product.
- Use the logo in a way that suggests the Nightfix project made, endorses or supports your product or service.
- Call a modified version "Nightfix" when you distribute it. Rename it and say it's based on Nightfix.

If you're not sure, open an issue and ask.
